QR Code Maker

Make the QR code for the links page, the app link or WhatsApp booking. You can edit the links at any time without reprinting the QR code.

  • Scan the code with an iPhone and an Android phone first.
  • Give the printer the SVG. It stays sharp at any size. Use the PNG for social posts and screens.
  • Print it at least 2.5 cm wide (5 cm or more on posters and roll-ups), with a white margin around it.
  • The QR code holds the page address, not the links. Changing links in config.js never needs a reprint. Only changing the address does.
